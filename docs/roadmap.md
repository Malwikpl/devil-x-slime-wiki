# Roadmap

Devil x Slime started with the Heavenly Dragons because they're the most iconic powers in DxD. The goal is the whole DxD
world. These are plans, not promises, and the order can change.

## Done

- **Boosted Gear**: Boost, Explosion, Transfer, Dragon Shot, three forms, body strain
- **Ddraig** as your partner, and his Bargain
- **Balance Breaker**: the red Scale Mail, with its rage meter
- **Ascalon** and its ruined churches
- **Juggernaut Drive** and the Longinus Smasher
- **Divine Dividing**: the wings, flight and Divide
- **Divine Dividing's Balance Breaker**: the white Scale Mail, Half Dimension, Compression Divider
- **The White Dragon Emperor** boss and his colosseum
- **Divide in the Boosted Gear**
- **Penetrate**
- Smooth models (with the classic look kept), cutscenes, callouts and effects
- **[The Devil race](races/devil.md)**: five classes from Low-class to Satan-class, bat wings, Demonic Power, the magic
  circle, night and day, and **Holy Water**
- **[The Peerage](races/peerage.md)**: High-class Devils are **Kings** with 15 Evil Pieces that make a player, a
  beaten monster or an otherworlder their servant, each piece with its strengths, **Promotion** for Pawns, and magic
  circles to call each other
- **[Rias Gremory](races/rias.md)**: an NPC King in the old school building who recruits you into her peerage, so
  single-player worlds get it too, and whose flyer can save you from death
- **Divine Dividing gets stronger**: [Reduce](divine-dividing/index.md#reduce),
  [Reflect](divine-dividing/balance-breaker.md#reflect), a finishing compress for Compression Divider, and
  [rival fights](divine-dividing/index.md#rival-fights) against the Boosted Gear
- **[Angels and Fallen Angels](races/angels.md)**: two races with feathered wings that grow with rank, spears of
  light, Blessing, the Great Spear, sin and the fall, and hybrids when a Fallen Angel takes an Evil Piece
- **[Stray Devils](world/stray-devils.md)** (0.1.72): Devils twisted by the Evil Piece they stole, fighting the way
  their piece does, rising on dark nights, and ruled from ruined mansions by named beasts, Viser first. The Stray Devil
  mark now means something
- **[Rias's hunts](world/stray-devils.md#riass-hunts)** (0.1.72): she sends her servants after Strays and erases them
  with her Power of Destruction
- **[Divine Dividing's Juggernaut Drive](divine-dividing/juggernaut-drive.md)** (0.1.72): the white dragon, the white
  Longinus Smasher, and **Transfer overload** (Noroi's idea) for the Boosted Gear
- **[Twilight Healing](twilight-healing/index.md)** (0.1.77): Asia's healing Sacred Gear, with Touch, Healing Arrows
  and the Healing Field
- **[Sacred Gears only in humans](getting-started/first-gear.md#only-in-humans)** (0.1.84), as in the story
- **Noroi's gear and race batch** (0.1.84): the Boost's speed, Ddraig's fire, tougher Scale Mails, a white Scale Mail
  that halves magic and projectiles, and mastery for the race skills, with an Angel's
  [gold Great Spear](races/angels.md#mastery) and the Devils' [Demonic Blast](races/devil.md#mastery-and-the-demonic-blast)
- **[Brave Saints](races/brave-saints.md)** (0.1.84): the Angels' cards, Poker and Blackjack hands, and Heaven's
  Judgement
- **Faster wings** (0.1.84): the race wings drawn from the graphics card, so a Seraph's twelve no longer cost frames
- **[Durandal and the Holy Factor](durandal/index.md)** (0.1.92): Xenovia's holy sword, sealed in a crypt under one church
  in three, with a ground shockwave and a tamed cut through any defence
- **[Akeno's Thunderlight](races/holy-lightning.md)** (0.1.92): mastery and five modes for Holy Lightning, with the
  Thunderlight Blast and Storm
- **[Structures on solid ground](world/structures.md#where-they-stand)** (0.1.92): dry, level land, clear of other
  structures, other mods' included
- **A bigger Longinus Smasher** (0.1.92), twice as wide for both dragons
- **[Nekomata and Nekoshou](races/nekomata.md)** (0.2.0): Koneko's and Kuroka's race, with ears and tails, Senjutsu, Kasha,
  Youjutsu's Poison Mist, Mud Bind, Veil and Mirage, and Malice
- **[Sword Birth](sword-birth/index.md)** (0.2.0): Kiba's Sacred Gear, fading copies of swords and blades from the ground
- **[Lore mode](lore-mode.md)** (0.2.0): a game rule for each world that makes the Longinus gears as strong as in the
  story

- **[House powers](races/houses.md)** (0.2.13): a pure-blooded Devil is born of House Bael, Gremory, Phenex, Sitri or Leviathan, by a
  roll: the Power of Destruction, wings of fire and rebirth, water in living shapes, or the raw sea and a Sea Dragon form
- **[Forbidden Balor View](balor-view/index.md)** (0.2.13): Gasper's Sacred Gear, a real time stop for what you look at, everything in
  sight or everything around you
- **[A Juggernaut Drive that breaks through](boosted-gear/juggernaut-drive.md#the-dragon)** (0.2.13): both dragons break what would stop
  them as they walk or fly
- **[Living wings](reference/looks.md#living-wings)** (0.2.13): the race wings move with you and beat with the flight, with softer lines
  on the feathered ones
- **[Light weapons](races/light-weapons.md)** (0.2.33): Angels and Fallen Angels shape their light into a sword and a bow, and can keep the Light Spear in the hand
- **[The Castle Town of Gremory](world/castle-town.md)** (0.2.33): a walled town in Hell with a castle, people in its streets, guards, a market and an inn, under a purple sky
- **[Promotion's armour forms](boosted-gear/promotion-forms.md)** (0.2.33): Illegal Move Triaina's Knight, Rook and Bishop forms of the Scale Mail for a Boosted Gear Pawn, and the
  crimson True Queen with all three at once. A player's suggestion
- **[Growing into your Pawns](races/peerage.md#growing-into-your-pawns)** (0.2.33): a Longinus holder on 8 Pawns starts with few awake, and wakes the rest as their body grows stronger
- **[Stronger pieces in Lore mode](lore-mode.md#the-evil-pieces)** (0.2.33): Evil Pieces that give as much as they do in the story, like a Rook's monstrous strength
- **[The Boost by press in the Scale Mail](boosted-gear/balance-breaker.md#the-boost-inside)** (0.2.33): each press a stack at once, as far as your max EP reaches
- **[A living Scale Mail](boosted-gear/balance-breaker.md#the-wings-and-the-tail)** (0.2.33): wings from the anime that lag, beat and ripple, and a tail that follows you

## Next up

- **The rest of the Castle Town**: the bounty board, with Stray Devil bounties for coins, EP and renown, then the castle's lords, titles and the treasury.

## After that

- **An NPC Seraph** for single player, who recruits you into her deck as Rias does for Devils.
- **The Joker** (Dulio's wild card), together with **Mutation Pieces**.

## Between the big items

- **More bosses** to fight, alongside the White Dragon Emperor.
- **Fenrir**, the god-slaying wolf: only a Divine Dividing holder in Juggernaut Drive can Divide it down into a smaller,
  weaker wolf you can tame.
- **Holy swords**: Excalibur's fragments next, then Ex-Durandal.
- **More Sacred Gears**, one at a time.
- **Annihilation Maker**: Leonardo's Longinus, which creates monsters.
- **Sword of Betrayer**: Kiba's Balance Breaker for Sword Birth, with holy-demonic swords and his Sword Knights.
- **Forbidden Invade Balor the Beast**: Forbidden Balor View's Balance Breaker.
- **Named demonic swords from the story** for Sword Birth, like Holy Eraser and Flame Delete, each with its own model.
- **Rias's peerage as NPCs**, starting with Asia.

## Maybe later

- Bloodline powers for pure Angels and Fallen Angels, as the Devil Houses have
- Physical Evil Pieces: the pieces as real items you can hold, not only picked from a menu
- Rating Games
