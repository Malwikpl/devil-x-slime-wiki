# Devil x Slime

<div class="hero" markdown>
![Devil x Slime logo](assets/images/logo.png)

**High School DxD comes to Tensura: Reincarnated.** Devil x Slime is an addon for the Tensura: Reincarnated mod
(Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge). It started with the most iconic powers in DxD, the two Heavenly Dragons' Sacred Gears,
and now has Asia's Twilight Healing, Kiba's Sword Birth, Gasper's Forbidden Balor View, Xenovia's Durandal, the three
races of the Three Factions (Devils with their Houses and Evil Pieces, Angels with their Brave Saints, and Fallen
Angels), Koneko's cat Youkai and the Stray Devils to hunt. It is growing into the whole DxD world.
</div>

## What's in the mod

    ---

-   :material-eye:{ .lg .middle } **[Forbidden Balor View](balor-view/index.md)**

    ---

-   :material-fire:{ .lg .middle } **[House powers](races/houses.md)**

    A pure-blooded Devil is born of House Bael, Gremory, Phenex, Sitri or Leviathan: the Power of Destruction, wings of
    fire and rebirth, water in living shapes, or the raw sea.

-   :material-chess-queen:{ .lg .middle } **[The Peerage](races/peerage.md)**

    ---

    High-class Devils are Kings with 15 Evil Pieces. Make a friend, a beaten monster or an otherworlder your servant,

-   :material-account-tie:{ .lg .middle } **[Rias Gremory](races/rias.md)**

-   :material-spear:{ .lg .middle } **[Angels and Fallen Angels](races/angels.md)**

    ---

    Feathered wings that multiply with rank, spears of light, Blessing and the Great Spear. Sin makes an Angel fall,

-   :material-cards-playing:{ .lg .middle } **[Brave Saints](races/brave-saints.md)**

-   :material-crown:{ .lg .middle } **[The White Dragon Emperor](world/white-dragon-emperor.md)**

    ---

    A boss in a ruined white colosseum. Beat him for Albion's jewel.

-   :material-sword:{ .lg .middle } **[Ascalon](boosted-gear/ascalon.md)**

    ---

    Saint George's dragon-slaying sword, waiting in a ruined church. It can live inside the Boosted Gear.

-   :material-sword:{ .lg .middle } **[Durandal](durandal/index.md)**

    ---

    Xenovia's holy sword, sealed in a crypt, for those with the Holy Factor. Its wave tears along the ground, and once
    tamed it cuts through anything.

-   :material-book-open-page-variant:{ .lg .middle } **[Lore mode](lore-mode.md)**

    ---

    A game rule for one world: the Longinus gears as strong as in the story, with Boost and Divide by press and no

## Quick start

1. **Install** the mod and its requirements on the client and the server: see [Installation](getting-started/installation.md).
2. **Reincarnate as a human** and roll the Boosted Gear, Divine Dividing, Twilight Healing, Sword Birth or Forbidden Balor View as
   your Unique Skill (only one Sacred Gear each, and [only humans](getting-started/first-gear.md#only-in-humans) roll them).
3. **Put the skill on an ability slot** in Tensura's Status Menu (default **B**) and press the slot key (**Z**, **X** or
   **C**) to summon your gear.
4. **Use it.** Every use earns mastery, and mastery unlocks the rest. [Your first Sacred Gear](getting-started/first-gear.md)
   walks you through it.

!!! tip "New here?"
    Start with [Your first Sacred Gear](getting-started/first-gear.md), then read the page for the gear you rolled. The
    [FAQ](faq.md) answers the questions players ask most. Want to play a Devil? See [Devils](races/devil.md). An Angel or a
    Fallen Angel? See [Angels and Fallen Angels](races/angels.md). A cat? See [Nekomata and Nekoshou](races/nekomata.md).

## Good to know

- Everything in this wiki describes the **latest version** of the mod. Server owners can change almost every number
  in the [configuration](reference/configuration.md); the numbers here are the defaults.
- [Lore mode](lore-mode.md) is off unless you turn it on. These pages describe the normal game.
- The mod is built for **Tensura: Reincarnated 2.0.1.3 or newer** on Minecraft **1.21.1** with **NeoForge**.
- There are hidden **easter eggs** for multiplayer. No spoilers here.
