# Penetrate

Ddraig's oldest power. With the **Boosted Gear Gift** (full mastery), the gear's blows go through defences.

## What it goes through

Every hit of the gear penetrates: your fist in the gauntlet and in the Scale Mail, the Ascalon blade, Dragon Shot, and
Juggernaut Drive's claws, shockwave and Longinus Smasher. They ignore:

- Tensura's **Resistances and Nullifications**: Physical Attack Resistance on Charybdis or a Metal Slime, Magic
  Resistance on Hinata, a slime player's Physical Attack Nullification.
- **Barriers** up to Guardian: Multilayer Barrier, Reflector's Full Counter (it can't throw your hit or Dragon Shot
  back), Guardian.
- **Barrier domes** and the Barrier and Magic Barrier spells **break** at the first penetrating hit. Your own dome never
  does.

## What still holds

- Armour.
- Dodging.
- Pride, Strengthen Body and Anti-Skill.
- Infinite Regeneration's death save.
- The [White Dragon Emperor](../world/white-dragon-emperor.md)'s cap of 300 damage a hit.

Held weapons, Transfer and other skills never penetrate.

## The price

A hit that actually breaks through something costs **0.5% of your max magicules**, once per tick, so a blast through
several resistant mobs is one charge. It's free in Juggernaut Drive. If you can't pay, the hit lands as if Penetrate
were off.

## The switch

Penetrate is the skill's **Passive Ability** toggle in Tensura's Status Menu. Reaching the Gift switches it on once,
with Ddraig's lesson; after that it's your choice. The Sacred Gear panel shows when it's on: the form row reads
"Penetrate" in crimson.

## The show

A crimson crack where the hit breaks through, and **PENETRATE!** with its own sound, at most every 3 seconds.
