# Divide (Albion's power)

In the story, Issei took Vali's jewel into his gauntlet. Here, a Boosted Gear holder who takes **Albion's power** can
Divide too, but only in the red Scale Mail, and only at a price.

## Getting it

Beat the [White Dragon Emperor](../world/white-dragon-emperor.md) and take **Albion's jewel** while you wear the
[Scale Mail](balance-breaker.md). Hold use on the jewel for 5 seconds.

A short cutscene plays: the blue jewel is driven into the forearm's, a burst of light and green shards, a dome of light,
the armour's jewels flickering blue, then the left forearm turns white and the gauntlet calls "Vanishing Dragon Power Is
Taken". **Sneak** skips it.

You never get Divine Dividing itself, and Tensura's copying skills don't copy this power.

## The mode

**Divide** (the Boosted Gear's mode 6) joins the mode key's cycle once you have the power.

- Outside the Scale Mail it says "Only in the Scale Mail."
- In Juggernaut Drive's dragon the keys stay the dragon's (see [In the dragon](#in-the-dragon)).
- While Divide is on one of your first three ability slots, the Scale Mail's **left forearm is white with Albion's blue
  jewel**, and everyone sees it. It flashes white as it changes.

## The strike

Your left hand strikes what you look at, up to **8 blocks** away, and lands one Divide:

- its **attack halves for 30 seconds** (not its speed);
- **half its magicules and aura flow into yours** (what doesn't fit is lost);
- a Boosted Gear enemy loses a held stack.

There is no bonus to your attack and no mastery: Boost stays the gear's strength.

**Once per enemy.** While an enemy is still divided (by you or anyone), it can't be divided again. After its 30
seconds, it can. A boss that refuses effects is remembered, so it is still only once. An enemy with over ten times your
max EP is beyond you.

## The price

!!! danger "Each Divide costs life"
    Each Divide takes **2% of your max health**, which grows back **1% every 10 minutes** of play.

- It counts toward [Juggernaut Drive's](juggernaut-drive.md#the-price) half. At that floor Ddraig refuses ("Your life
  can't pay for another Divide, partner."), and he refuses Drives too while your Divides fill it.
- Becoming a dragon race clears it along with the Drive's debt, and so does `/dxdtensura lifespan clear`.
- Misses and refusals cost nothing.
- There are 5 seconds between Divides.
- Creative pays nothing.

## In the dragon

In Juggernaut Drive, the power divides **attacks** instead, with no life price:

- shots and spells on course to hit the dragon within 8 blocks shrink to a white spark and vanish (a friend's shots,
  your pets' and thrown tridents fly on);
- every other hit lands at **half** (except the void and `/kill`);
- "Divide! Divide!" plays, and the dragon's wing jewels flare bright blue each time it divides, fading back to green.

## The panel

In the Divide mode, the panel shows Divide's key and your life cost, like "Life -16% (6% heals)": what your life
carries, and the part still growing back.

!!! note "Operators and testing"
    `/dxdtensura albion give [players]` gives the power without the fight, and `/dxdtensura albion take [players]`
    removes it.
