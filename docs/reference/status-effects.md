# Status effects

The effects Devil x Slime adds, as they appear in your inventory.

| Effect | Where it comes from | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Transferred Boost** | A Boosted Gear holder's [Transfer](../boosted-gear/modes.md#transfer) | Half the Boost's extra power: everything you damage takes ×(1 + ½ × (2ⁿ − 1)), where n is the level, for 30 s |
| **Balance Breaker** | Wearing the red [Scale Mail](../boosted-gear/balance-breaker.md) | Shows the transformation (Tensura counts it as one) |
| **Juggernaut Drive** | Being the [dragon](../boosted-gear/juggernaut-drive.md) | Shows the transformation |
| **Shortened Lifespan** | The [Juggernaut Drive's price](../boosted-gear/juggernaut-drive.md#the-price) | Lower max health. It follows your life debt, so `/effect clear` doesn't remove it; a dragon race or `/dxdtensura lifespan clear` does |
| **Spent Dragon** | The end of a Juggernaut Drive | Max magicules and aura halved for 2 minutes |
| **Divided** (I to III) | Being divided by [Divine Dividing](../divine-dividing/index.md#divide) | Every hit it deals (melee, skills, magic, projectiles) halved once per level; lasts 30 s after the last Divide |
| **Absorbed Power** | Dividing an enemy with Divine Dividing | The strength you took from Divides, up to ×8 your own (×16 in the white Scale Mail, ×32 in the white dragon) while it lasts |
| **Reduced** | Divine Dividing's [Reduce](../divine-dividing/index.md#reduce) | Each second you lose the Divine Dividing holder's attack and 2% of your max spiritual health, and nothing heals you. Lasts 10 s |
| **Compressed** | [Compression Divider's compress](../divine-dividing/balance-breaker.md#compression-divider) | Magicules and aura held at 5% of max while you stay Divided |
| **Divine Dividing Scale Mail** | Wearing the [white Scale Mail](../divine-dividing/balance-breaker.md) | Shows the transformation |
| **Juggernaut Drive (Vanishing Dragon)** | Being the [white dragon](../divine-dividing/juggernaut-drive.md) | Shows the transformation |
| **Devil's Night** | Being a [Devil](../races/devil.md#night-and-day) at night or in Hell | Demonic Power hits 25% harder; magicules regenerate 25% faster |
| **Under the Sun** | Being a [Devil](../races/devil.md#night-and-day) in the daytime sun (open sky, no rain) | 10% slower; your class's attack bonus is gone |
| **Heaven's Grace** | Being an [Angel](../races/angels.md#day-and-hell) under the open daytime sky | Light Spear hits 25% harder; magicules regenerate 25% faster |
| **Hell's Weight** | Being an [Angel](../races/angels.md#day-and-hell) in Tensura's Hell | 10% slower |
| **Heaven's Hand** | Playing a [Brave Saints](../races/brave-saints.md#playing-a-hand) hand | The hand's power, for 30 s |
| **Mud Bound** | Standing in a Nekomata's [Mud Bind](../races/nekomata.md#mud-bind) | You can barely move or jump, but can still swing and cast |
| **Veil** | A Nekomata's [Veil](../races/nekomata.md#veil) | You're invisible, though armour still shows, and mobs lose you unless they come within 3 blocks |
| **Touki** | A [Nekomata's Senjutsu](../races/nekomata.md#touki) | Harder bare-handed blows, less damage taken and 5% more speed while it lasts |
| **Consumed by Malice** | [Malice](../races/nekomata.md#malice) reaching 100 | The berserk: for 15 s no skills or race key, and you strike the nearest living thing on your own |
| **Flames Stilled** | A holy hit on a [House Phenex](../races/houses.md#house-phenex) Devil | No regeneration or rebirth for 10 s. A new holy hit starts the time again |
| **Water Wrapped** | A [House Sitri](../races/houses.md#house-sitri) Devil's Water Serpent | You can barely move or jump, but can still swing and cast, and it can't be drunk away with milk |
| **Crushing Depths** | A [House Leviathan](../races/houses.md#house-leviathan) Devil's Crushing Depths | 30% slower and barely able to jump, for 1 s |
| **Sea Dragon** | Being a House Leviathan Devil's [Sea Dragon](../races/houses.md#the-sea-dragon) | Shows the form |
| **Enhancement** | A [Valkyrie's](../races/valkyrie.md#enhancement) Galdr | +10% to +30% attack and speed by her rank, for 60 s (75 mastered) |
| **Rune Sealed** | A [Valkyrie's](../races/valkyrie.md#rune-seal) Rune Seal | You can barely move or jump, flight is off, and your skills and spells are refused; you can still swing |
| **Tipsy** | A bottle of [Sake](../races/valkyrie.md#sake) | Hiccups for 2 minutes. A genius Valkyrie's Full Burst turns drunken: more circles, all her magicules, then paralysis |
| **Fox Fire** | A [Kitsune's](../races/kitsune.md#fox-fire) blue fire | The blue burn: 1 to 16 damage a second by the fox's rank for 4 s (6 mastered), fire-immune or not; water puts it out |
| **Bewitched** | A [Kitsune's](../races/kitsune.md#youjutsu) Bewitch | The mob fights for the fox until it ends, then is its old self again |

Juggernaut Drive also leaves vanilla and Tensura effects behind: Weakness II, Slowness II, Mining Fatigue, Fragility and
Paralysis. See [After it](../boosted-gear/juggernaut-drive.md#after-it).
