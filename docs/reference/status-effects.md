# Status effects

The effects Devil x Slime adds, as they appear in your inventory.

| Effect | Where it comes from | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Transferred Boost** | A Boosted Gear holder's [Transfer](../boosted-gear/modes.md#transfer) | Half the Boost's extra power: everything you damage takes ×(1 + ½ × (2ⁿ − 1)), where n is the level, for 30 s |
| **Balance Breaker** | Wearing the red [Scale Mail](../boosted-gear/balance-breaker.md) | Shows the transformation (Tensura counts it as one) |
| **Juggernaut Drive** | Being the [dragon](../boosted-gear/juggernaut-drive.md) | Shows the transformation |
| **Shortened Lifespan** | The [Juggernaut Drive's price](../boosted-gear/juggernaut-drive.md#the-price) | Lower max health. It follows your life debt, so `/effect clear` doesn't remove it; a dragon race or `/dxdtensura lifespan clear` does |
| **Spent Dragon** | The end of a Juggernaut Drive | Max magicules and aura halved for 2 minutes |
| **Divided** (I to III) | Being divided by [Divine Dividing](../divine-dividing/index.md#divide) | Every hit it deals (melee, skills, magic, projectiles) halved once per level; lasts 30 s after the last Divide |
| **Absorbed Power** | Dividing an enemy with Divine Dividing | Your strength from Divides: ×2, ×4 or ×8 (up to ×16 in the white Scale Mail) while it lasts |
| **Reduced** | Divine Dividing's [Reduce](../divine-dividing/index.md#reduce) | Each second you lose the Divine Dividing holder's attack and 2% of your max spiritual health, and nothing heals you. Lasts 10 s |
| **Compressed** | [Compression Divider's compress](../divine-dividing/balance-breaker.md#compression-divider) | Magicules and aura held at 5% of max while you stay Divided |
| **Divine Dividing Scale Mail** | Wearing the [white Scale Mail](../divine-dividing/balance-breaker.md) | Shows the transformation |
| **Devil's Night** | Being a [Devil](../races/devil.md#night-and-day) at night or in Hell | Demonic Power hits 25% harder; magicules regenerate 25% faster |
| **Under the Sun** | Being a [Devil](../races/devil.md#night-and-day) in the daytime sun (open sky, no rain) | 10% slower; your class's attack bonus is gone |
| **Heaven's Grace** | Being an [Angel](../races/angels.md#day-and-hell) under the open daytime sky | Light Spear hits 25% harder; magicules regenerate 25% faster |
| **Hell's Weight** | Being an [Angel](../races/angels.md#day-and-hell) in Tensura's Hell | 10% slower |

Juggernaut Drive also leaves vanilla and Tensura effects behind: Weakness II, Slowness II, Mining Fatigue, Fragility and
Paralysis. See [After it](../boosted-gear/juggernaut-drive.md#after-it).
